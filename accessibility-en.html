Accessibility statement

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This is a translation. The binding version is the Hebrew statement.

General

JUD Studio is committed to providing equal service to all clients and visitors, including people with disabilities. We have worked to make this website accessible, comfortable and easy for everyone to use.

The website has been made accessible in accordance with Israel’s Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 5773-2013, and Israeli Standard IS 5568, which is based on WCAG 2.0, at conformance level AA.

Accessibility adjustments on the website

  • The language of each page is declared, and words in another language are marked so screen readers pronounce them correctly.
  • The whole website can be used with the keyboard alone (Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, Esc and the arrow keys). The element in focus has a clearly visible outline.
  • A “Skip to main content” link appears at the top of every page.
  • Pages are structured with hierarchical headings and regions (navigation, main content, footer) to help screen reader users.
  • Images have alternative text, and purely decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Buttons that show only an icon have a text description. Links that open in a new tab are announced as such.
  • Pop-up windows (the project gallery, the enlarged image and the menu) manage focus, close with the Esc key and return focus to where you were.
  • Text and background colours meet the AA contrast requirements.
  • The page can be zoomed up to 200% in the browser without losing content, and the website is adapted for mobile phones.
  • The website respects the “reduce motion” setting of your operating system. The slideshow at the top of the page has a pause button.
  • Background music is off by default and only plays when you press the music button, which also mutes it at any time.
  • Client reviews are shown one at a time and do not change automatically. You move between them with the arrow buttons or the keyboard arrow keys, and the new text is read out by screen readers.
  • The before-and-after comparison can be operated with the arrow keys.

The accessibility menu

An accessibility button in the corner of the screen opens a menu with the following options:

  • Larger and smaller text.
  • Readable font: replaces the decorative font with a simple, readable one.
  • High contrast: light text on a black background, without textures.
  • Highlight links and buttons.
  • Stop animations: stops all movement on the website, including smooth scrolling, animations and slideshows.
  • Reset all settings.

These settings are saved in your browser for your next visit.

Known limitations

  • The website links to external services (WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook). Their accessibility is the responsibility of the companies that run them.
  • Some images in the project galleries are described in general terms (project name and image number) rather than with a detailed description of their content.

We keep improving the accessibility of the website. If you run into any difficulty, please let us know.
