Privacy policy

This is a translation. The binding version is the Hebrew policy.

General

JUD Studio respects the privacy of visitors to its website. This document explains what information is (or is not) collected when you use the website, in accordance with Israel’s Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, and its amendments.

What information is collected

  • The website has no forms, registration or personal area, and does not collect your name, phone number, email address or any other personal information.
  • The website does not use cookies, analytics tools, social media pixels or targeted advertising.
  • All website files (images, fonts and code) are loaded from the website itself, not from third-party servers.

Accessibility menu settings

If you choose settings in the accessibility menu (for example larger text or high contrast), they are saved only in your browser (Local Storage) so they apply on your next visit. This information is not sent to us and does not identify you. You can delete it at any time with the “Reset” button in the accessibility menu or in your browser settings.

Contacting us on WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook

The WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook links take you to external services, and contacting us through them is also subject to those services’ privacy policies. The information you send us (such as your name, phone number and project details) is used only to answer your enquiry and provide our services, and is not passed on to third parties.

Client reviews

The reviews shown on the website are published with the clients’ consent and display a shortened name only.
